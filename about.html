17 is Right Here

17 Is Right Here is the first best album by SEVENTEEN. It was released on April 29, 2024 with "Maestro" serving as the album's title track. The physical album comes in six versions: Here, Hear, Dear, Deluxe, KiT and Weverse Album.

Music Show wins:

  1. Show Champion: May 8, 2024
  2. M Countdown: May 9, 2024
  3. Music Bank: May 10, 2024
  4. Show! Music Core: May 17, 2024

Tracklist:

  • Maestro
  • Lalali (Hip-Hop team)
  • Spell(Performance team)
  • Call Call Call! (Korean Ver.)
  • Happy Ending (Korean Ver.)

"It's an album that contains SEVENTEEN's signature sound and showcases their growth as artists."

-S.Coups, special interview to Spotify
